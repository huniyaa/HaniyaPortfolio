Project Overview

Description

The Supreme Court of Canada Case Visualizer is a web-based data visualization tool designed to make Supreme Court case information more accessible and easier to explore over time. The project presents complex legal data through a clear, interactive timeline interface, allowing users to browse cases by year and identify patterns at a glance rather than through dense text.

Problem

Information about Supreme Court cases is often presented in text-heavy, non-visual formats, making it difficult for non-experts to explore trends or understand historical context. Existing resources prioritize completeness over clarity, creating barriers for students, researchers, and the general public.

Goals

To translate case data into a clear, visual timeline, designed to reduce cognitive load. The system prioritizes scalability and clarity, allowing new data to be added without compromising usability.

Role

I was responsible for the front-end and interaction design, focusing on layout, hierarchy, and how users navigate and interpret the data. All development and data implementation were handled by the collaborator.
